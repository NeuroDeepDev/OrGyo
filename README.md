OrGyo is a recommendation and discovery platform designed to help students find the best college or organization according to their personal preferences and requirements.

Instead of searching through many websites and comparing information manually, a student can enter what matters to them — fees, ranking, specialization, location, placements, opportunities, and student feedback — and OrGyo helps identify suitable organizations.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to choose the right college or organization because information is scattered across different websites and platforms.

For example, a student may want:

- A college within a particular budget
- A specific course or specialization
- A college with good rankings
- Better placement opportunities
- A preferred location
- Specific facilities or opportunities
- Feedback from existing students

OrGyo aims to bring these factors together and make the decision-making process easier.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

OrGyo lets users search for organizations and compare them based on the factors that matter most to them.

A student provides their preferences, and the system filters, ranks, or recommends organizations that best match those preferences.

### Example

A student could enter:

```text
Course: Computer Science
Budget: ₹1–2 lakh/year
Location: Bangalore
Priority: Placement + Ranking
```

And OrGyo would return a filtered, ranked list of matching colleges/organizations.

## ✨ Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🔍 **Search & Filter** | Filter organizations by fees, ranking, specialization/branch, location, and more |
| 🖥️ **Interactive Listings** | Engaging, easy-to-navigate organization profiles |
| 📊 **Placement Insights** | Placement records and opportunities for each organization |
| 💰 **Fee Transparency** | Clear fee structure per course/program |
| 💬 **Student Feedback** | Verified students can leave independent feedback with a simple two-mood rating (👍 Good / 👎 Bad) |
